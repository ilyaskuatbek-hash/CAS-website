Understanding the strands

Creativity, Activity, and Service explained.

CAS is often misunderstood because the three strands are broad. Each one includes many possible ways to contribute, learn, and grow.

Creativity

Creativity is about expression, exploration, and innovation.

Creativity does not only mean art. It can include music, writing, dance, photography, coding, design, cooking, problem-solving, or building something new. The main idea is that the student creates, experiments, or expresses ideas in a meaningful way.

A creative CAS project may involve starting a blog, composing a song, creating a short film, designing a poster campaign, or building a school event that showcases student talent.

🎨

Examples

Painting murals, learning a musical instrument, photography projects, writing workshops.

Activity

Activity is about physical engagement and personal challenge.

Activity encourages students to stay active, develop discipline, improve their physical and mental well-being, and step outside their comfort zone. This can be through sport, fitness, dance, hiking, martial arts, or regular exercise programs.

It is not just about being athletic — it is about commitment, consistency, and growth through movement and effort.

🏃

Examples

Joining a sports team, practicing yoga, training for a run, organizing fitness sessions for peers.

Service

Service focuses on helping others and making a positive impact.

Service is about contributing to communities and addressing real needs. It may involve volunteering, supporting vulnerable groups, helping younger students, organizing charity events, or working on environmental or social projects.

Good service work is thoughtful, responsible, and meaningful. It should have a positive effect on others and reflect empathy and initiative.

🤝

Examples

Teaching children, volunteering at shelters, environmental cleanups, community food drives, tutoring classmates.

Putting it together

CAS works best when the strands overlap.

🎭

Creative service

Organizing a school arts event for younger students.

🏀

Active service

Running a sports camp for children in the community.

📚

Creative activity

Creating educational materials and leading a literacy club.

Final idea

CAS is about balance, reflection, and meaningful action.
