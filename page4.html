A chance to explore

CAS is a fun way to try something new.

You do not need to be an expert or have everything figured out. CAS gives you space to explore an interest, take on a new challenge, meet people, and discover what you enjoy along the way.

Start with curiosity

Small first steps can lead to big discoveries.

🎨

Try a creative hobby

Learn photography, make a short film, try baking, write a story, or design something for your school.

🚴

Challenge yourself to move

Test a new sport, go hiking, join a dance class, or set a personal fitness goal and track your progress.

🌱

Get involved in your community

Help with a school event, tutor someone, volunteer locally, or take part in an environmental project.

No perfect starting point

You can begin with what you already enjoy—or try the opposite.

CAS is not about choosing the most impressive activity. It is about being curious, making a genuine effort, and learning from the experience. You might build on an existing interest or take a step outside your comfort zone.

It is okay if your first idea changes. Trying, reflecting, and adjusting are all part of discovering what matters to you.

🧭

Follow your curiosity

Notice what you enjoy, what you wonder about, or what you want to improve.

👥

Bring someone along

Invite a friend or join a group to make a new experience less intimidating.

📓

Reflect as you go

Think about what surprised you, what challenged you, and what you learned.
