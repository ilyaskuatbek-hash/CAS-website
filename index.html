The three pillars

CAS balances personal development and community contribution.

🎨

Creativity

Creativity involves exploring new ideas, expressing original thinking, and developing artistic, imaginative, or innovative skills.

🏃

Activity

Activity promotes physical well-being, discipline, and healthy habits through sports, movement, teamwork, and personal challenge.

🤝

Service

Service encourages students to contribute meaningfully to others, solve real-world problems, and act with empathy and responsibility.

Why CAS matters

It is more than a requirement — it is a life skill.

CAS is designed to help IB students become thoughtful, compassionate, and active participants in their communities. It encourages reflection, initiative, and a stronger understanding of the world beyond academics.

Through CAS, students connect learning with action, turning knowledge into meaningful experiences that shape values, confidence, and purpose.

🌱

The IB approach

CAS helps students develop a balanced and purposeful identity.

1. Intentionality

Students choose activities that match their interests, values, and goals.

2. Reflection

Students think critically about what they learned, how they grew, and what changed.

3. Action

They turn ideas into effort and lead projects, events, or service initiatives.

4. Impact

CAS promotes meaningful contributions that benefit people, communities, and society.
